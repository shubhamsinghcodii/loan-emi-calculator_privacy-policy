Privacy Policy

Loan EMI Calculator · Effective 29 September 2026

1. Overview

Moonknight Studios built the Loan EMI Calculator app as a free, ad-supported utility. This policy explains what data the app and its advertising partners handle. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. Your loan data stays on your device

Calculations, history entries and saved loans are stored only in a local database on your phone. We do not operate servers, we do not create accounts, and we never upload your loan figures anywhere. Clearing the app's data (or uninstalling) permanently deletes everything.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and share:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • Approximate location, app interaction and diagnostic data
  • Cookies or similar storage for ad personalisation and measurement

You can reset your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Consent choices are collected through Google's consent form on first launch (and applied per region, e.g. EEA/UK). Learn more at Google's advertising technology page and the AdMob privacy guidance.

4. Permissions used

  • INTERNET — required to load ads and the consent form.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used to check connectivity before ad requests.

No location, contact, storage, camera or microphone permission is requested.

5. Children

The app is a general-purpose financial calculator, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; parents or guardians may contact us to request deletion of any such data.

6. Data safety & retention

Because loan data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to retain, share or sell. Ad-related data is handled by Google under its own policies linked above. PDF reports you export are saved to the app's private cache and can be deleted at any time from your device.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example, new features or ad partners). The effective date at the top will always show the latest version.

8. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Reach Moonknight Studios through your app store listing's support contact.
